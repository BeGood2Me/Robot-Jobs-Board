Robot Jobs Board — off-site impressions checklist
=================================================

Run weekly after the board ingest deploys. Prefer hub URLs (homepage, /companies/{slug},
/locations/united-states-robotics-jobs) over deep job URLs.

1) Social (3–5 posts / week)
   - LinkedIn + X: share US or company hubs with framing like
     "Live robotics jobs aggregated from public ATS boards — US / UK / remote."
   - Example: https://www.robotjobsboard.com/locations/united-states-robotics-jobs
   - Example: https://www.robotjobsboard.com/companies/figure
   - Example: https://www.robotjobsboard.com/

2) Niche links (1–2 solid placements beat mass directories)
   - Robotics Discord / Slack career channels: pin or post the US + remote hubs.
   - University robotics club career pages: offer a "live robotics jobs" link.
   - Niche job/tool directories that accept aggregators (only reputable ones).

3) After posting
   - Note the URL and date here or in your notes.
   - Recheck GSC impressions in 2–4 weeks for company + location hubs.
